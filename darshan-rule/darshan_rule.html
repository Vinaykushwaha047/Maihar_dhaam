Darshan Rules

A Guide for a Peaceful and Respectful Visit

Follow these general guidelines to help maintain cleanliness, devotion and a peaceful atmosphere during your visit to Maa Sharda Temple, Maihar.

Explore Guidelines ↓
TEMPLE ETIQUETTE

Guidelines for Devotees

Make your darshan experience meaningful by respecting the temple environment, fellow devotees and temple staff.

🙏
01

Maintain Devotion

Maintain a calm and respectful attitude inside the temple. Avoid shouting, pushing or creating unnecessary disturbances.

👕
02

Dress Appropriately

Wear clean and respectful clothing suitable for a place of worship. Follow any dress instructions displayed by the temple.

📵
03

Mobile & Photography

Keep your phone on silent. Before taking photographs or videos, check the current temple restrictions and follow staff instructions.

🧹
04

Keep the Temple Clean

Use designated dustbins. Do not litter, damage temple property or leave offerings in places where they are not permitted.

🚶
05

Follow the Queue

Wait patiently in designated queues. Avoid pushing and allow elderly devotees and people needing assistance to follow the arrangements.

🛡️
06

Follow Safety Instructions

Follow notices, barricades and instructions from temple staff. Do not enter restricted areas or leave children unattended.

BEFORE YOUR VISIT

Plan Your Darshan

A little preparation can make your temple visit smoother and more comfortable.

  • Check current darshan timings before travelling.
  • Allow extra time during Navratri and festival days.
  • Carry essential belongings and drinking water where permitted.
  • Follow the latest instructions issued by temple authorities.
  • Use designated routes and facilities for visitors.
🪷

श्रद्धा और विश्वास

Visit with devotion, patience and respect. Help make the temple experience peaceful for every devotee.
