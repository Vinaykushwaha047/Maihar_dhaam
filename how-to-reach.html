How to Reach Maihar

Maihar is well connected by air, rail and road. Choose the most
convenient way to reach and plan your visit to the divine abode of Maa Sharda.

By Air

The nearest airport to Maihar is Jabalpur Airport (JLR), located around 120 km away. From the airport, you can hire a taxi, book a cab or take a bus to reach Maihar.

⌖ Nearest Airport: Jabalpur (JBP)

↟ Distance: 120 km (approx.)

↗   Search Flights

By Train

Maihar Railway Station (MYR) is well connected with major cities like Bhopal, Jabalpur, Delhi, Mumbai and Varanasi. You can book tickets through IRCTC and take a local auto, taxi or bus to reach the temple.

⌖ Maihar Railway Station (MYR)

↗ Connected with major cities

↗   Book Train Ticket (IRCTC)

By Road

Maihar is easily accessible by road from nearby cities. You can travel by bus, car or taxi. Regular buses are available from Jabalpur, Rewa, Katni and other nearby towns.

⌖ Well connected via NH & state roads
